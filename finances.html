Budget Items
How the rule works
50% Needs — essentials you can't skip: housing, food, utilities, transport, insurance, and minimum debt payments.

30% Wants — everything that improves life but isn't required: dining out, subscriptions, travel, upgrades.

20% Savings — investments, emergency fund, and any debt payment above the minimum. This is the bucket to protect first.
